# Preparing and sending a proposal

Shaoor-AI Construct | Project Proposals

Turn an enquiry you've already received into a personalised, branded proposal you can share with the customer — available on Growth and Enterprise plans.

## 1. Prepare a draft

Open **Enquiries**, click into the enquiry you want to quote, and select **Prepare proposal** in the sidebar. This creates a draft that:

- Carries over the customer's name and contact details from the enquiry.
- Fills in a plain-language **requirements summary** built from the questions they answered — anything they left blank is marked "requires clarification" rather than guessed at.
- Suggests a few of your existing projects that look relevant, with a short reason for each.

If a draft already exists for that enquiry, you're taken straight to it — this never creates a duplicate.

## 2. Edit the content

On the draft's page you can edit everything the customer will see:

- **Title, reference, introduction** — the framing before the details.
- **Requirements summary** — edit the generated text freely; your edits are never overwritten.
- **Scope of work, exclusions, assumptions** — be specific here. Anything you don't cover is, by default, not included.
- **Portfolio** — add, remove or reorder the suggested projects, or add others from your own portfolio. Client names and budgets from a project are never shown to the customer, even if you've filled them in.
- **Testimonials** — optionally attach one or more approved testimonials.
- **Timeline and pricing** — both are entirely your call. Choose "to be discussed," a fixed amount, or an indicative range; nothing is ever generated automatically.
- **Internal notes** — visible only to your team, never to the customer.

Use **Preview** anytime to see exactly what the customer will see. Previewing never counts as a customer "open."

## 3. Approve and publish

When you're ready, select **Approve & publish**. This is the one step that makes the proposal accessible outside your dashboard — a draft is never visible to anyone until you do this.

Only Owners and Admins can publish, revoke or republish. Editors can prepare and edit drafts but not publish them.

## 4. Share the link

Once published, use **Copy link** or **Share via WhatsApp** to send it yourself — share it through whichever the customer marked as their preferred contact method (shown on the enquiry). The system never sends this automatically.

**Treat the link as a private document.** Anyone who has it can open the proposal — there's no separate login. Don't post it anywhere public, and only share it with the customer directly.

## 5. Track activity

The proposal's page shows:

- Whether and how many times the link has been opened, and when it was last opened. This means the link was accessed — it isn't proof a particular person read it.
- Any **"Discuss this proposal"** or **"Request a site visit"** message the customer sent, with their preferred date/time if given. These are requests, not confirmed bookings — follow up with the customer directly to confirm.

## 6. Making a change after publishing

Edit the draft as usual, then select **Approve & publish** again. This creates a new, dated revision on the *same link* — the customer doesn't need a new URL, and the page shows the revision date so it's clear when it was last updated. Anything already sent to a customer stays exactly as they saw it until you republish.

## 7. Revoking a proposal

Select **Revoke** to immediately disable the link — useful if it was sent to the wrong person, or the deal has moved on. Revoking doesn't delete the proposal; you can review it later, and republishing brings the same link back online.

## A note on your plan

Project Proposals is included on Growth and Enterprise plans. If your workspace is on Free or Starter, any proposals already published stay reachable at their existing links, but preparing, editing or publishing new ones is disabled until you upgrade.
