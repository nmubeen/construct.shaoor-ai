# Sharing project progress privately with a customer

Shaoor-AI Construct | Private Project Progress

Give a customer their own private page showing how their project is coming along — current stage, milestones, dated updates and approved photos — without publishing anything to your public website. No customer login is required; access is controlled entirely by a private link.

## 1. Create a project

Open **Project progress** in the sidebar and select **New project**. This is separate from a public portfolio project — nothing here ever appears on your website. Fill in:

- **Project title** and **customer display name**.
- **Customer email/phone** — private, never shown to the customer.
- **Location** — off by default; tick "Show location to the customer" only if you want it on their page.
- **Customer-facing summary**, **internal notes** (staff-only).
- Optional links to the **enquiry**, **proposal** or **public portfolio project** this came from — purely for your own reference, never exposed.
- Planned start/target completion dates — always shown to the customer as *planned*, not a guarantee.

## 2. Milestones

Add milestones from the project workspace — title, optional description, planned date, status (Not started / In progress / Completed). Reorder with the up/down arrows. The customer sees "3 of 8 milestones completed" rather than an unexplained percentage.

## 3. Progress updates

Select **New update** to start a draft. On the update's own page:

- Fill in work completed, work in progress, what's next, and an optional honest note about any delay or issue.
- Add photos — drag and drop, or use **Choose photos** (opens your phone's camera/gallery on mobile). Reorder or remove any before saving. A failed upload never blocks the rest — retry it or move on.
- You can also attach an existing public portfolio image instead of uploading — the page makes clear that image is already public, not newly private.
- **Save draft** at any point.

An **Owner or Admin** reviews and selects **Publish update** to make it visible to the customer. Editors can prepare and edit drafts but can't publish. A published update can still be edited by an Owner/Admin afterwards, or pulled back with **Withdraw update** (the history is kept, just no longer shown).

## 4. Publishing the project summary and milestones

Editing the project's summary or milestones only changes your own working copy — **the customer never sees a change until an Owner/Admin selects "Publish changes"** on the project workspace page. A banner always shows when there are unpublished changes, so it's never ambiguous what the customer currently sees versus what's still pending. Use **Preview** to see the current draft before publishing.

## 5. Sharing the link

Only Owners and Admins can manage customer access, from the project workspace's **Customer access** panel:

- **Generate link** — creates a private link. The full link is shown to you exactly once, right after you generate or rotate it — copy it immediately, it isn't stored anywhere you can retrieve it again later.
- **Rotate link** — replaces the current link with a new one; the old one stops working immediately.
- **Revoke access** — disables the link immediately without deleting anything. You can revoke a link even if your plan no longer includes this feature.
- Optional expiry, in days.

**Anyone who has the link can open the page — there's no separate login.** Treat it like a private document: don't post it anywhere public, share it only with the customer directly.

## 6. What the customer sees

Your logo and branding, the project's stage and milestones, planned dates (clearly labelled as planned), the latest update first with a readable history, approved photos, what's next, and a way to contact you directly. Never shown: internal notes, private contact details, unpublished changes, or any other customer's project.

## 7. Project lifecycle

Move a project through **Planned → Active → On hold → Completed → Archived** from the workspace page. **Archiving revokes customer access automatically** (and keeps the full history). **Completing a project does not revoke access** — the customer keeps their link until you explicitly revoke it, it expires, or your plan no longer includes this feature.

## A note on photos

Uploaded photos are stored privately — never on a public web address. The customer page only ever shows them through a temporary, time-limited link generated after checking that whoever is asking still has valid access. Photos you take on a phone often carry GPS location data in their file — this is automatically stripped before storage.

## A note on your plan

Private project progress pages are included on Growth and Enterprise plans. If your workspace is on Free or Starter, existing projects and their history stay intact, and you can still revoke an active customer link — but creating, editing, uploading or publishing is disabled, and the customer page shows a simple "currently unavailable" message (it never reveals anything about your billing) until you upgrade.
