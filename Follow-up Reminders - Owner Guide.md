# Remembering what to do next

Shaoor-AI Construct | Follow-up Reminders

Every enquiry and proposal can carry its own reminders — "call tomorrow," "check back after their decision date," "confirm the site visit." These are internal notes to your team only; the system never emails, texts or messages a customer automatically.

## 1. Scheduling a follow-up

Open any **Enquiry** or **Proposal** and scroll to the **Follow-ups** section. Select **+ Schedule a follow-up** and fill in:

- **Action** — a short title (e.g. "Call about the site visit").
- **Note** — optional, internal only.
- **Assign to** — yourself or any active, non-Viewer teammate. Defaults to you.
- **Due** — pick **Tomorrow**, **In 3 days** or **Next week**, or set an exact date and time. The exact resulting date and time is always shown before you save, in your workspace's own timezone — nothing is ever set to a silent midnight.

You can schedule a genuinely past due time if you mean to (useful for logging something you already did) — you'll see a warning, but it's never changed for you.

## 2. Managing an existing follow-up

Each follow-up has **Complete**, **Reschedule**, **Edit** and **Cancel**:

- **Complete** — optionally record an outcome ("Customer confirmed the site visit for Thursday"). Cancelled or already-completed items can't be completed again.
- **Complete & schedule next** — closes this one out and immediately schedules the next step in one action, so you never lose track between calls.
- **Reschedule** — change just the due date/time.
- **Edit** — change the title, note, assignee or due time.
- **Cancel** — removes it from your open work without deleting its history.

Nothing is ever deleted — completed and cancelled follow-ups stay visible on the enquiry or proposal as a record of what happened.

## 3. The Follow-ups dashboard

Open **Follow-ups** in the sidebar for a single view across every enquiry and proposal:

- **My follow-ups** / **All follow-ups** toggle.
- Filters for **Overdue**, **Due today**, **Upcoming**, **Completed** and **Cancelled**, plus assignee, date range and a search box.
- An **Overview** panel showing how many are overdue, how many are due today, and your own next few.
- Quick **Complete**, **Reschedule** and **Cancel** right from the list — click through to the enquiry or proposal for the full edit form or **Complete & schedule next**.

A red badge on the **Follow-ups** sidebar item shows your current overdue count.

## 4. On a proposal's page

A proposal's Follow-ups section also shows any follow-ups scheduled from its *linked enquiry*, clearly labelled **From the enquiry**, so you have the full picture without leaving the page. These are read-only here — manage them from the enquiry itself.

## 5. Reminders are in-app only

Overdue and due-today counts update live, on the dashboard and in the sidebar, whenever you're using Construct. **Nothing is sent while you're away** — no email, SMS, WhatsApp or push notification. If a reminder matters enough that you need to be alerted outside the app, set a reminder in your own calendar or phone as well.

## 6. Assigning to teammates

Only active team members who aren't Viewers can be assigned — a Viewer has no way to act on a follow-up, so they're not offered. If someone assigned to a follow-up later leaves the workspace or is changed to Viewer, that follow-up is flagged **Needs reassignment** rather than silently left with someone who can no longer act on it — reassign it to keep it moving.

## 7. Timezone

Your workspace has its own timezone, set on **Settings → Workspace identity**. Every due date, the "today"/"overdue" boundary, and the date shortcuts all use this timezone — not your browser's or the server's. If your team works across regions, set this to wherever your office or projects are based.

## A note on proposals and your plan

Follow-ups on an **enquiry** are always available. Follow-ups on a **proposal** follow the same plan rule as proposals themselves (Growth and Enterprise plans) — if your workspace doesn't currently have that entitlement, existing proposal follow-ups stay visible and you can still complete or cancel them, but scheduling new ones or changing their due date is disabled until you upgrade.
